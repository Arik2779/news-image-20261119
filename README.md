The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png`
to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a newspaper front page for Thursday
19 November 2026: masthead "WORLD NEWS" with the date, and four illustrated
panels, each verified against reporting from 19 November 2026 (see
`../RESEARCH.md`):

1. **TRUMP, XI, PUTIN MEET IN SHENZHEN — FIRST-EVER TRILATERAL SUMMIT
   TODAY.** The first-ever meeting of the sitting presidents of the US,
   China and Russia, on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen; agenda
   includes nuclear arms (after New START expired in February 2026),
   Iran, Ukraine, trade and AI. A separate Trump–Putin bilateral is also
   planned for the same day. No agreements are expected — the image shows
   the meeting, not a deal.
2. **GTA VI OUT TODAY — 13-YEAR WAIT OVER AS GAME LAUNCHES WORLDWIDE.**
   Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November in
   each market, starting with New Zealand, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox
   Series X|S; set in Vice City. Review embargo lifted 18 November with
   a Metacritic score of 99; about 15.9 million digital preorders,
   about $1.5 billion in pre-release sales.
3. **CYCLONE AKVAN BEARS DOWN — RED ALERT ON INDIA'S EAST COAST,
   LANDFALL DUE FRIDAY.** Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of
   Bengal; IMD's 02:30 IST 19 November bulletin, red alert for coastal
   Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha, extremely heavy rain forecast for
   19–20 November, schools shut in six Odisha districts. Landfall is
   forecast for Friday 20 November between Narsapur and Puri — the image
   says "due Friday", not that it has landed.
4. **INDIA–JAPAN 2+2 TALKS IN TOKYO — DEFENCE MINISTERS MEET ON
   INDO-PACIFIC.** The 4th India–Japan Foreign and Defence Ministers'
   Dialogue (2+2) in Tokyo on 19 November: Rajnath Singh and S. Jaishankar
   with Shinjiro Koizumi and Toshimitsu Motegi, on defence cooperation,
   maritime security and a free and open Indo-Pacific.
